Flow stress vs strain rate (power-law relation): σ = Kε˙^m
Where: 
- σ = flow stress (MPa) at a chosen strain
- ε˙= strain rate (1/s)
- K = material strength coefficient
- m = strain-rate sensitivity exponent

Because the relationship is nonlinear, the code converts it to a log–log linear equation: ln(σ) = m ln(ε˙) + ln(K)
So: 
- Slope = m
- Intercept = ln(K)
